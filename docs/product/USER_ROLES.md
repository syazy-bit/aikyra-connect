# User Roles

> Planned roles. No authentication or role system is implemented yet.

Aikyra defines seven user roles. Roles shape permissions, workflows, and matching behavior.

## 1. Citizen / Community Member

- Submits societal challenges from their community.
- Provides local context, photos, and location details.
- Validates that proposed solutions address real needs.

## 2. University Student

- Joins projects as team members / contributors.
- Brings skills (technical, design, research) used in matching.
- Builds prototypes under faculty mentorship.

## 3. Faculty / Mentor

- Provides domain expertise and guides student teams.
- Declares research areas and lab capabilities (used by the matching engine).
- Reviews and validates problem understanding and solution feasibility.

## 4. University Administrator

- Represents a university on the platform.
- Approves institutional participation in projects.
- Manages departments, labs, and official capabilities data.

## 5. Industry / Startup / MSME

- Offers technology, funding, mentorship, or manufacturing capability.
- Partners with universities to scale validated solutions.
- May sponsor challenges aligned with their CSR or product goals.

## 6. Government / Department

- Publishes departmental challenge calls and priorities.
- Validates challenges within their jurisdiction.
- Consumes impact reports for policy and funding decisions.

## 7. Platform Administrator

- Manages users, roles, and institutional verifications.
- Moderates content and resolves disputes.
- Maintains platform health and data quality.
