# Aikyra — Project Overview

> Status: planning document. Nothing described here is implemented yet unless explicitly noted.

## What is Aikyra?

**Aikyra — "Many Minds. One Impact."** is a collaborative societal innovation platform connecting citizens and communities with universities, students, researchers, industry, startups, and government stakeholders to transform real-world societal challenges into measurable solutions.

## The Problem

- Citizens report problems (water scarcity, waste, healthcare access) with no structured path to solutions.
- Universities and students have expertise and capacity but lack a pipeline of real problems.
- Industry can help scale but has no visibility into grassroots challenges.
- Government departments lack measurable impact data from ground-level innovation.

## The Vision

Every validated societal challenge becomes a well-understood problem (**Problem DNA**), is intelligently matched to the right collaborators, moves through a guided project lifecycle, and produces measured, verifiable impact.

## Core Workflow

```
Challenge
  → AI Understanding
  → Problem DNA
  → Validation
  → Intelligent Matching
  → University + Industry Collaboration
  → Project
  → Prototype
  → Deployment
  → Impact Measurement
```

## Guiding AI Principle

AI assists with understanding and recommendations. AI does not independently make final institutional or project decisions. Human validation remains part of the workflow at every critical step.
